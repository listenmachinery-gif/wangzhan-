# About ZYRON — Factory Photo Shot List

The first release uses only current ZYRON website photographs. The items below
are the missing evidence photographs recommended for the next content update;
they must be photographed at the real ZYRON facility and must not be replaced
with competitor or AI-generated factory evidence.

For every shot, retain useful machine context, remove temporary clutter during
background cleanup without changing the equipment, export a high-resolution
master and a web-optimized WebP/AVIF, and include a visible ZYRON logo or
traceable ZYRON production context whenever practical.

## Delivery guidelines

- Shoot landscape and vertical alternates when the activity is important on
  mobile.
- Leave safe negative space around the subject; avoid ultra-wide distortion.
- Record location, machine model, order reference, photographer, and capture
  date in the asset log.
- Do not publish a Customer inspection photo without written permission.
- Do not publish Container loading imagery if the machine, crate, and container
  scale are inconsistent with the real shipment.
